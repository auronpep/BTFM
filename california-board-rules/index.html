Rule Library

Issue-specific California prompts

charitable solicitation

Charitable Solicitation

Fundraising is not only a development activity. It can create board-level compliance and reputation risk.

  • A commercial fundraiser, fundraising counsel, commercial coventurer, or online platform is involved.
  • The campaign targets California residents and registration status is uncertain.

compensation approval

Compensation Approval

Compensation decisions need a disciplined process before the board reaches a number.

  • The compensated person is a founder, director, officer, key employee, relative, or related party.
  • The board lacks comparability data or is unsure who is disinterested.

conflicts of interest

Conflicts of Interest

A conflict policy is only useful if the board follows it before the decision is made.

  • A director, officer, founder, executive, relative, donor, landlord, or vendor may benefit.
  • The board is unsure whether disclosure, recusal, comparability, or approval standards apply.

minutes records

Minutes and Corporate Records

Minutes are evidence of board action, not a transcript of everything said.

  • The board cannot prove whether a motion passed or who was authorized to act.
  • A director, member, regulator, donor, lender, or litigant requests records.

statement of information

Statement of Information

The Statement of Information keeps public officer, address, and agent information current. The board should know who owns it.

  • The public record names the wrong officer, address, or agent for service of process.
  • The corporation may be suspended, forfeited, merged, dissolved, or inactive.

Escalation Pattern

When a boardroom question becomes a legal question

The practical signal is not whether a topic sounds legal. It is whether the board is about to rely on a threshold, exception, filing status, conflicted vote, public disclosure, donor restriction, contract, investigation, or enforcement deadline.

Legal Escalation

When to call counsel

Call counsel before the board acts when the decision depends on facts this field manual cannot verify.

  • The board is near a filing, audit, registration, or solicitation deadline.
  • A director, officer, executive, donor, or vendor may have an interest in the decision.
  • The organization is delinquent, suspended, under inquiry, or unsure which filings are current.
  • Minutes, bylaws, policies, or past practice do not clearly support the proposed action.
