Session design

What a practical session can include

The session can be tailored for new directors, a full board retreat, an audit or governance committee, or a leadership group preparing for a serious annual cycle.

  • Board packet review and question practice
  • Strategy, Safety, Audit decision paths
  • Committee and officer authority boundaries
  • Minutes-ready motions, recusals, abstentions, and follow-up
  • When to pause training and call counsel
