The training model

Three jobs every serious board has to practice

Strategy

What decision is the board making?

Directors learn to separate updates from decisions, ask for options, and connect agenda items to mission, budget, authority, and follow-through.

Safety

What risk needs board attention?

The session helps boards spot safety, insurance, personnel, donor-restriction, reporting, and internal-control issues that should not be buried in staff reports.

Audit

What evidence will show the board did its job?

Training connects financial oversight, Form 990 awareness, committee work, minutes, recusals, and packet review to practical board behavior.
