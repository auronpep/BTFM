What the webinar covers

A practical agenda for working-board directors

  1. Read the board packet for decisions, not background noise.
  2. Ask Strategy questions before approving budgets, plans, or major changes.
  3. Ask Safety questions about insurance, controls, youth protection, people, and reporting.
  4. Ask Audit questions about evidence, minutes, Form 990, financial review, and committee follow-up.
  5. Know when the issue has moved from education to specific legal advice.
