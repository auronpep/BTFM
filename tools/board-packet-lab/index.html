Use Boundary

Packet Modes

Choose the review mode before reading

Skim mode is for routine reports. Standard mode is for normal votes. Deep review is for material money, audit, executive, risk, conflict, insurance, and governing-document decisions.

Skim

Spot what changed

Use for monitoring reports where no board action is requested.

Standard

Confirm decision support

Use for ordinary approvals with clear motion language and complete attachments.

Deep Review

Test assumptions and authority

Use when the decision affects money, risk, filings, executives, policies, or records.

Guides

Packet guide by agenda item

deep-review

Audit Review Packet Guide

What directors should look for before audit committee or full-board audit review.

Expected packet items

  • Auditor communication or draft report
  • Management letter or note that none was issued
  • Management response to findings
  • Committee recommendation to the board

If missing, ask

  • What did the auditor raise that the board should understand?
  • What is management's response and timeline?
  • What needs to be recorded in the board minutes?

deep-review

Budget Approval Packet Guide

What a mature board should expect before approving or materially revising a budget.

Expected packet items

  • Proposed budget with prior-year comparison
  • Current year-to-date financials
  • Narrative assumptions for revenue, staffing, programs, reserves, and restrictions
  • Material risks or tradeoffs

If missing, ask

  • What changed from the prior approved budget and why?
  • Which assumptions would materially affect this budget if wrong?
  • Does the board need to approve a reserve use, restricted-fund plan, or changed program commitment?

standard

Executive Director Report Packet Guide

How directors can read executive reports for decisions, risks, and board-level exceptions.

Expected packet items

  • Executive summary with key changes
  • Metrics tied to board-approved priorities
  • Exceptions requiring board attention
  • Requested decisions or direction

If missing, ask

  • What changed since the prior report?
  • Which items need board action rather than awareness?
  • What risk or tradeoff should the board understand?

deep-review

Insurance and Safety Packet Guide

Packet expectations for board review of insurance, safety exposure, and risk transfer.

Expected packet items

  • Current insurance summary
  • Renewal changes, exclusions, or premium shifts
  • Known incidents, claims, or near misses
  • Management recommendation and unresolved questions

If missing, ask

  • What exposure changed since the last renewal?
  • What exclusions or gaps should the board understand?
  • Is any legal, HR, or claims advice needed before action?

Prep Windows

What to do before the meeting

one-week

Prepare for Audit Review

A one-week preparation window for audit committee or board audit review.

  • Confirm who reviewed the audit before the full board.
  • Read auditor communications and any management letter.
  • Identify board follow-up for findings, internal controls, or management response.

Decision: Accept the audit, assign follow-up, or request additional professional input.

48-hours

Prepare for a Budget Approval

A 48-hour preparation window for budget votes.

  • Read the executive summary before the spreadsheet.
  • Mark the three largest changes from the current or prior budget.
  • Check whether assumptions, reserves, restricted funds, and staffing are explained.

Decision: Approve, amend, defer, or request a revised budget package.

one-week

Prepare for a Conflict Vote

A one-week preparation window for conflict-sensitive board decisions.

  • Identify the interested person and nature of the interest.
  • Confirm what documents the board will rely on.
  • Plan how recusal, abstention, and vote result will be recorded.

Decision: Approve only after proper process, defer for advice, or decline.

24-hours

Prepare for an Executive Director Report

A 24-hour preparation window for turning executive updates into oversight.

  • Circle decisions requested from the board.
  • Mark material changes in finances, staffing, programs, compliance, or risk.
  • Write one question about assumptions or exceptions.

Decision: Determine whether the report needs action, monitoring, or no board decision.

48-hours

Prepare for Insurance Review

A 48-hour preparation window for insurance and safety review.

  • Review coverage summary, exclusions, deductibles, and renewal changes.
  • Ask whether incidents, claims, or new activities changed exposure.
  • Identify follow-up for broker, counsel, or management.

Decision: Approve renewal direction, assign follow-up, or request additional professional input.
