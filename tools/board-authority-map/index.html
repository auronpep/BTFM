Board Authority Map

A tool for separating board authority, officer authority, executive/staff authority, and delegated limits.

  • Output Authority boundaries for board, officers, committees, executive, staff, and volunteers.
  • Review Attorney review flagged

Inputs Needed

  • Bylaws
  • Delegation policies
  • Committee charters
  • Executive job description

Documents

  • Board Authority Map

Use This When

Use this when the board is confused about what belongs to the board, the chair, officers, committees, executive leadership, staff, or volunteers.

Questions to Ask

  • What has the board reserved to itself?
  • What has been delegated, and where is that delegation written?
  • Which director behaviors bypass management or board process?

Minutes Should Show

  • Any delegation, authority limit, or policy approval.

Red Flags

  • Individual directors direct staff or bind the organization without board action.

When to Call Counsel

Call counsel when bylaws, officer authority, director disputes, or corporate authority are unclear.

  • The board and executive disagree about who has authority.
  • A director is acting outside board-approved authority.
