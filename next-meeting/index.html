Pick the problem on the next board agenda.

Choose the agenda item that is making the meeting serious. Each prompt is designed to help a director prepare before the packet becomes a vote.

Budget approval Audit or Form 990 Executive director report Compensation Conflict of interest Insurance/safety Bylaws or policy changes Difficult board member issue Annual meeting

What to ask before the vote

Budget approval

Ask this
What changed from the prior plan, and what is the board authorizing management to do?
Documents to open
Proposed budget, year-to-date actuals, variance notes, cash forecast.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Audit or Form 990

Ask this
What did the reviewer flag, and what governance answer will become public?
Documents to open
Draft audit, management letter, Form 990 governance section, committee notes.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Executive director report

Ask this
Which items require board judgment instead of passive receipt?
Documents to open
ED report, dashboard, strategic plan, prior board assignments.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Compensation

Ask this
Who is disinterested, what comparability data exists, and what should the minutes show?
Documents to open
Compensation benchmark, conflict disclosures, executive session notes.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Conflict of interest

Ask this
Who has a financial or personal interest, and what process protects the decision?
Documents to open
Conflict policy, disclosure form, board roster, proposed transaction terms.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Insurance/safety

Ask this
Does the risk system match the actual programs, people, facilities, and incidents?
Documents to open
Insurance summary, incident log, safety policy, vendor or program reports.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Bylaws or policy changes

Ask this
What authority is changing, and does the board know the consequence before voting?
Documents to open
Current bylaws, redline, policy draft, vote threshold notes.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Difficult board member issue

Ask this
Is this a conduct, confidentiality, authority, attendance, or conflict problem?
Documents to open
Bylaws, code of conduct, attendance record, relevant correspondence.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Annual meeting

Ask this
What must be elected, approved, disclosed, or recorded before the year turns?
Documents to open
Agenda, board terms, officer slate, annual calendar, prior minutes.

Minutes should show the motion, material abstentions or recusals, documents reviewed, and the final action taken.

Use the same discipline for every serious agenda item.

1

Name the decision

If there is no decision, ask why the item is taking board time.

2

Find the authority

Check bylaws, policies, prior votes, committees, and delegated limits.

3

Build the record

Make sure minutes show the action, documents, conflicts, and vote result.

When the issue has legal edges, stop treating it as a template problem.

Conflicts, compensation, bylaws, corporate records, safety failures, and governance disputes can require legal judgment. Use this field manual for education, then call counsel when the facts matter.
