# Annual Governance Calendar

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Quarter 1

- Prior-year financial close.
- Audit or financial-review planning.
- Conflict disclosures.
- Committee work plans.

## Quarter 2

- Budget assumptions.
- Insurance renewal preparation.
- Program and risk review.
- Board training needs.

## Quarter 3

- Budget approval.
- Audit or review follow-up.
- Form 990 planning if applicable.
- Bylaws and policy review.

## Quarter 4

- Annual meeting preparation.
- Board evaluation.
- Officer and committee appointments.
- Governance calendar approval for next year.
