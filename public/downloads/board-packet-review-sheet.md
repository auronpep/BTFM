## Red Flags

- Late packet for a material vote.
- Approval requested without the document to approve.
- Conflict, compensation, bylaws, safety, personnel, or filing issue handled informally.

## Minutes Note

The minutes should identify:

- The exact action approved.
- The document version relied on.
- Vote result, abstentions, and recusals.
- Follow-up assignment.
