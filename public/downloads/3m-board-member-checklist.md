# $3M Board Member Checklist

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Before the Meeting

- What decision is the board being asked to make?
- What changed materially in budget, staffing, programs, risk, or compliance?
- Which packet documents support the decision?
- What should the minutes show if the board approves the item?

## Money and Audit

- Budget-to-actual variances reviewed.
- Reserve, restricted-fund, and cash-flow issues identified.
- Audit or financial-review follow-up assigned.
- Form 990 or public-disclosure issues scheduled when relevant.

## Risk and Safety

- Insurance renewal, claims, exclusions, and gaps reviewed on schedule.
- Safety, personnel, youth protection, and internal-control issues escalated.
- Professional advice requested when facts or legal duties matter.

## Authority and Records

- Board, officer, committee, executive, staff, and volunteer authority boundaries are clear.
- Motions identify the exact action and document version approved.
- Recusals, abstentions, conflicts, and follow-up assignments are recorded.
