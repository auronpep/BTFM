# Audit Committee Readiness Checklist

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Structure

- Audit committee or review body identified.
- Independence questions reviewed.
- Timeline set before the audit cycle begins.
- Auditor communication path is clear.

## Packet

- Draft audit or report.
- Auditor communication.
- Management letter or confirmation that none was issued.
- Management response and timeline.
- Committee recommendation for the board.

## Questions

- What should the full board understand?
- What findings require management follow-up?
- What should the minutes show?
- Does the organization need legal, accounting, or governance advice before board action?
