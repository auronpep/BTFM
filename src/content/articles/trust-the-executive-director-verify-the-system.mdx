Trusting the executive director does not mean the board stops looking. It means the board looks at the right things: the system, the authority, the reporting, and the risk signals.

The rule is that oversight should be structured enough that no one has to rely only on personality. A strong executive director should be able to show the board what is going well, what is changing, what needs a decision, and what could become a risk.

The board should not turn meetings into management reviews of every operational detail. It should insist that reports identify decisions, exceptions, trends, and issues that belong at the board level. That is how the board supports management without disappearing.
