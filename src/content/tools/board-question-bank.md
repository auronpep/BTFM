## Use This When

Use the question bank when a board packet gives information but does not make the boardroom judgment obvious. Pick one or two questions. Do not turn the meeting into cross-examination.

## Question Discipline

A good board question is short, answerable, tied to the board's role, and focused on a decision, risk, assumption, or record.
