## How to Use the Lab

Start with the agenda item, not the thickest packet section. Write the decision in one sentence, then find the documents that support that decision. If the decision cannot be stated plainly, the board is not ready to vote.

## Three-Pass Review

1. Skim for the requested action, date, amount, policy, contract, or filing.
2. Mark missing assumptions, unexplained changes, late documents, or unclear authority.
3. Write one oversight question and one minutes note before the meeting starts.

## Boardroom Rule

The board does not need to rewrite management's work. The board does need enough information to make a documented, good-faith decision.
