## The boardroom problem

The development team wants a campaign, platform, or paid fundraiser. The board hears revenue projections but not the compliance structure, donor representations, contract rights, or public-risk controls.

## The careful rule

California Attorney General materials describe charitable solicitation laws, professional fundraiser obligations, and newer platform rules. Which rule applies depends on who solicits, who receives or controls funds, what is promised, and where the solicitation reaches.

## What the board should ask

Ask who is registered, who controls donations, what contract terms protect the charity, what donors are told, and who files required reports.

## What not to do

Do not treat fundraising compliance as paperwork after launch. Do not let a vendor or platform define the organization's legal story without board oversight.

## When this becomes legal

This becomes legal when paid solicitors, platforms, commercial coventurers, restricted gifts, donor complaints, multistate activity, raffles, gaming, or emergency appeals are involved.
