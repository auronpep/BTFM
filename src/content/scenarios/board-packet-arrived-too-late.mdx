The board can respect operational urgency without pretending directors had adequate time. The practical move is to identify the real deadline and decide whether a narrower action is available.

If approval cannot be delayed, the minutes should not hide the pressure. They should show what the board reviewed, what authority was granted, and what follow-up is required.
