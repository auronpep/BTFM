---
title: "Late Board Packet"
description: "The packet arrives too late for directors to read before a material vote."
severity: "serious"
signal: "Directors receive a large or consequential packet shortly before the meeting."
whyItMatters: "Late packets can convert board approval into rubber-stamping and weaken the record of informed attention."
immediateBoardAction:
  - "Ask which items can be informational only."
  - "Defer material votes when directors lack enough time."
  - "Record follow-up when the board proceeds for a specific reason."
topics: ["tools", "next-meeting"]
useBefore: ["board-meeting"]
attorneyReviewRequired: false
---

Late packets are process signals, especially before money, audit, compensation, safety, conflict, or bylaws votes.
