# Legal Source Notes

Worker 7 source inventory for California Board Rules content. These notes support attorney review and should not be treated as legal advice.

## Source Priority

Use official sources first:

- California Attorney General charity pages, forms, FAQs, and Guide for Charities.
- California Secretary of State business entity pages for Statement of Information issues.
- IRS Form 990 pages, instructions, and annual filing guidance.

Secondary summaries should not be used for public legal claims unless an attorney has confirmed the point against current official sources.

## Current Public-Copy Boundaries

- Audit threshold pages say California Attorney General materials describe audit requirements for covered charitable corporations with gross revenues of $2 million or more. They do not decide coverage, revenue classification, exemptions, or prior-year compliance.
- Audit committee pages say Attorney General materials describe an audit committee requirement and membership limits for qualifying organizations. They do not decide whether a current committee satisfies the rule.
- Form 990 pages say exempt organizations generally must file annual returns, subject to exceptions, and that missed required filings for three consecutive years can cause automatic loss of tax-exempt status. They frame governance disclosures as board-review prompts.
- Registry pages distinguish California Attorney General Registry registration and renewal from IRS filing. They do not decide exemption status.
- Statement of Information pages rely on California Secretary of State FAQ language for periodic filing concepts and tell users to check the entity record directly.
- Conflict, compensation, minutes, and solicitation pages are issue spotters. They intentionally avoid definitive statements about validity, reasonableness, self-dealing, access rights, or enforcement consequences.
