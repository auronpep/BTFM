# Competition Review Package

## One-Sentence Pitch

Board Training Principles is a practical, legally literate training library that helps nonprofit boards move from good intentions to competent governance through articles, scenarios, downloads, webinars, and in-person training.

## Why This Direction Should Win

- It treats the project as a real resource library, not a decorative microsite.
- It preserves the strongest idea from the brainstorming site: boards need clarity, not just activity.
- It connects directly to NPO Lawyers without making the free site feel like a thin sales funnel.
- It turns the three source documents into a reusable curriculum and content engine.
- It is static-first and Hostinger-friendly.
- It is structured for parallel workers from the start.

## Boss Demo Flow

When presenting the finished prototype, use this order:

1. Homepage: show the four primary actions.
2. Boards 101: show the guided learning path.
3. Article page: show readable long-form education with related downloads.
4. Scenario page: show how real board problems are taught.
5. Downloads: show practical templates.
6. Training page: show free webinar and paid in-person paths.
7. Footer: show disclaimer and NPO Lawyers connection.

## Evaluation Scorecard

Use this to compare all four competing submissions.

| Category | Weight | What Good Looks Like |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Strategy fit | 20 | Clearly serves article reading, training explanation, webinar/in-person signup, and NPO Lawyers referral. |
| Content architecture | 20 | Turns source docs into a durable article/scenario/download system. |
| Visual credibility | 15 | Feels warm, serious, readable, and distinct from generic law firm sites. |
| Conversion clarity | 15 | CTAs are obvious without making the free resource feel manipulative. |
| Implementation realism | 15 | Can be built quickly and deployed on Hostinger with low operational risk. |
| Parallel execution | 10 | Work can be split safely across multiple workers. |
| Accessibility/SEO readiness | 5 | Uses semantic structure, contrast, focus states, metadata, sitemap, and clean URLs. |

## Non-Negotiables

- The homepage must show all four primary actions.
- The site must include educational-information and no-attorney-client-relationship disclaimers.
- Critical text must be readable and not embedded only in images.
- Articles must connect to practical next steps.
- Downloads must be easy to find.
- Training inquiry must be easy to find.
- NPO Lawyers must remain the legal services destination.

## Presentation Talking Points

- "This is not just a blog. It is a board training curriculum expressed as a public resource library."
- "The design borrows the trust signals of NPO Lawyers, but the user experience is built around learning."
- "The scenario format is the differentiator. It turns abstract fiduciary duties into boardroom judgment."
- "Astro static deployment keeps Hostinger launch simple and avoids unnecessary backend risk."
- "The worker plan lets us build content, design system, homepage, article templates, downloads, training pages, QA, and deployment in parallel."
