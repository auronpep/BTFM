# Mature Board Self-Assessment

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Rate each item: not yet, inconsistent, reliable.

## Strategy

- The board can name the three decisions that matter most this year.
- Reports are tied to goals, risk, budget, and material changes.
- Directors distinguish oversight from operational preference.

## Safety

- Insurance, youth protection, personnel risk, and internal controls are reviewed on a schedule.
- The board knows which risks require escalation.
- Safety concerns are documented without turning minutes into a claims file.

## Audit

- Financial statements, budgets, audit materials, and Form 990 are reviewed before approval.
- The board asks about deviations, assumptions, restrictions, and reserves.
- Minutes preserve exact decisions and packet references.
