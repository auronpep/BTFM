# Minutes Quality Scorecard

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Rate each item: missing, partial, clear.

## Action

- The motion is stated clearly.
- The approved document version is identified.
- The vote result is recorded.

## Process

- Conflicts, recusals, and abstentions are recorded.
- Executive session or sensitive matters are handled carefully.
- Follow-up assignments are clear.

## Judgment

- The minutes show the board acted, but do not become a transcript.
- Material reports or packet items are referenced.
- Legal-sensitive wording is reviewed before approval when needed.
