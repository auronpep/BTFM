# Board Authority Map

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Board

Acts collectively through meetings, motions, approvals, and delegated authority.

Boundary: individual directors do not manage staff or bind the organization alone.

## Officers

Carry out duties assigned by bylaws, board action, and approved policies.

Boundary: officers do not replace board approval where approval is required.

## Committees

Prepare recommendations, monitor assigned areas, or act only within delegated authority.

Boundary: committee authority should be written and reported to the board.

## Executive and Staff

Operate programs and management systems within approved plans, policies, and budgets.

Boundary: staff escalate material changes, risks, and reserved decisions to the board.

## Volunteers and Agents

Serve within written roles, supervision, and delegated limits.

Boundary: volunteers and agents do not bind the organization outside assigned authority.
