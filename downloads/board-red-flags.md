# Board Red Flags

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Urgent

- Approval requested without the document to approve.
- Conflict-sensitive decision handled informally.
- Safety, personnel, claim, or insurance issue minimized.

## Serious

- Late packet before a material vote.
- Material budget gap without explanation.
- Individual director acts outside board authority.

## Watch

- Reports are long but requested board action is unclear.
- Minutes tell the story but not the action.
- Annual obligations depend on memory instead of a calendar.

## Board Response

- Ask for the missing fact or document.
- Defer when the decision is not ready.
- Assign follow-up.
- Call counsel when legal rights, filings, disputes, conflicts, compensation, or safety exposure matter.
