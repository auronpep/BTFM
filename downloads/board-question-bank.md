# Board Question Bank

Educational draft. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Budget

- Which three lines changed the most, and why?
- Is this timing, assumption, control, or strategy?
- Does the board need to approve a revised budget or reserve use?

## Audit

- What did the auditor identify that the board should understand?
- What is management's response and timeline?
- What should be recorded in the minutes?

## Executive Oversight

- What decision or direction do you need from the board?
- Which risks or exceptions should we understand?
- What should come back next meeting?

## Legal Escalation

- Does this involve legal rights, filings, conflicts, compensation, bylaws, safety, or a dispute?
- Should the board defer until counsel reviews the facts?
